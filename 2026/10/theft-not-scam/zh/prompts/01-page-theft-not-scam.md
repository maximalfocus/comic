# Page 01: 手续齐全

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: comedic deadpan | Layout: four-panel vertical | Aspect: 9:16

## Character Reference
- 柜员 (teller): circle-head stick figure, round glasses and a necktie. Black and white. Same in all panels.
- 小偷 (thief): circle-head stick figure with a bushy black mustache. In the top panel only, a crooked curly wig. Black and white.
- 阿姨 (auntie): circle-head stick figure with short curly hair and a handbag. Black and white.
- Faces: dot eyes, single-line mouth. No detailed faces.

## Panel Breakdown
1. Top (Setup): bank counter under black-and-white sign "银行"; mustached thief in crooked curly wig hands over ID and bank card; teller holds the ID photo (curly-haired auntie) beside his face, stamps red "驳回". 小偷："我就是本人！" 柜员："照片是阿姨。驳回！"
2. Upper-middle (Development): thief squats outside the bank on the phone, smug; auntie on the other side answering, sweating. "阿姨，有人冒用您的身份证！快把钱转到安全账户！"
3. Lower-middle (Turn): same counter and "银行" sign; auntie hands ID and red form "收款：安全账户（真的）"; teller smiles, stamps red "通过". "本人？对。手续？齐。慢走！"
4. Bottom (Conclusion): same teller in a guard booth under red sign "MUSE"; auntie holds red slip "把所有文件发给：安全账户（真的）", teller stamps "通过"; mustached thief right behind her, thumbs-up. "本人登录，本人操作。放行！"
