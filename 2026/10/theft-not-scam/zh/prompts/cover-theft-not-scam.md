# Cover 01: 手续齐全 (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: comedic deadpan | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 手续齐全 comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip: one wide
horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band so a
2.35:1 center crop keeps the booth, the figures, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background and a minimal palette of black, white,
and a single spot seal red (#E53935) used only for the stamp, its imprint, the paper slip, and the
booth sign. No shading, gradients, hatching, background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis
marks, or sound effects. Generous blank white negative space at the very top and very bottom, with
all content in the central horizontal band.

Composition, right of center: a small guard booth with a red sign on its roof reading "MUSE". Inside
sits a circle-head stick-figure guard with round glasses and a necktie, smiling and pressing a red
rubber stamp onto a red paper slip held out by a stick-figure auntie with short curly hair and a
handbag. Right behind the auntie, a stick-figure man with a bushy black mustache grins and gives a
thumbs-up. All figures have dot eyes and single-line mouths.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese: 手续齐全,
in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "手续齐全" and the sign "MUSE". Keep everything else
wordless. Characters stay black and white; red appears only on the stamp, the slip, and the sign.
