# theft-not-scam

Idea: Muse 的门卫就像银行柜员，拦得住"偷"，拦不住"骗"。

## Shared thread
A gatekeeper that checks credentials and paperwork stops a stranger with a fake key, but waves
through the real owner who was talked into the request. The turn is the same counter approving
the victim, because every check passes. Orange marks concept objects only: cards, forms, stamp,
the Muse sign and request slip.

## How the two differ
- English: four roles — a masked thief with a crayon "REAL CARD", a separate sunglasses con artist
  posing as the bank, payee "TOTALLY NOT A SCAM LLC". Orange accent.
- Chinese: the thief and the con artist are one person — denied at the counter impersonating 阿姨 with her stolen ID and a crooked wig (人证不合一), he
  goes outside and phones 阿姨 to warn that someone is impersonating her ID — true, since it was him (“安全账户”), then stands behind her, the same mustache,
  as Muse's guard waves her request through. Seal-red accent, 驳回/通过 stamps.
