# Cover 01: 码农的「农」 (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 码农的「农」 comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip: one
wide, calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band so
a 2.35:1 center crop keeps the figures, the machines, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background and a minimal palette of black, white,
and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used only for the harvester and the AI screen. No shading,
gradients, hatching, background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects.
Generous blank white negative space at the very top and very bottom, with all content in the central
horizontal band.

Mirrored composition across the middle of the banner: in the center-left, a small row of ripe wheat
stalks with a simple orange combine harvester; in the center-right, a matching row of office desks
with laptops whose screens are orange. Between the two halves stands one simple circle-head stick
figure with round glasses, wearing a straw hat and holding a laptop under one arm, looking from the
wheat to the desks. Everything reads as one calm horizontal line of objects.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese: 码农的「农」,
in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "码农的「农」". Keep everything else wordless (the screens
are plain orange rectangles, no letters). Characters stay black and white; orange appears only on
the harvester and the screens.
