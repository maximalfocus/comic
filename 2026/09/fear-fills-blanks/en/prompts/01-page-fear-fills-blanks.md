# Page 01: The Quote Has Baggage

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: four-panel | Aspect: 4:3

## Character Reference
- Expert: circle-head stick figure, round glasses, holds a handheld microphone. Black and white.
- Survivalist: circle-head stick figure, striped pom-pom wool cap. Black and white.
- Inspector: circle-head stick figure, holds a clipboard. Black and white.
- Faces: dot eyes, single-line mouth. No detailed faces.

## Panel Breakdown
1. Top Left (Setup): podcast desk; Expert speaks beside a quote card whose black stem is followed by
   a large white blank. "AI could…"
2. Top Right (Development): storeroom with a bean pallet; Survivalist has just written an orange
   reading on a luggage-tag note and photographs card and note together.
   Card: "AI could…" / Note: "MY TAKE: empty stores."
3. Bottom Left (Turn): baggage carousel; the Survivalist's tag lies detached on the belt; Inspector
   holds the repost and clips on their own tag. Repost: "AI could empty stores." / Tag: "MY TAKE:
   jobs are next."
4. Bottom Right (Conclusion): podcast desk, bean wall; Expert holds the still-blank card beside the
   grown repost; Survivalist peeks over the beans. Repost: "AI could empty stores. Jobs are next." /
   "My unfinished sentence checked two bags."

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line art digital comic strip in a four-panel grid layout (2×2), landscape
4:3. The style is simplified cartoon illustration with clear black outlines on a pure white
background and a minimal color palette of black, white, and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used
only for key concepts. Characters are simplified stick figures with circle heads, dot eyes and
single-line mouths, distinguished only by props. Characters stay black and white; orange is used
only for the handwritten words that listeners filled in. Printed source words, the "MY TAKE"
headers, card and tag outlines, the phone, beans, carousel, and clipboard stay black and white,
and the blank space on the original card stays pure white. No shading, gradients, hatching,
background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Mostly blank white
negative space. The whole page is a fully opaque, solid pure white (#FFFFFF) background with no
transparency and no gray tint anywhere. Four equal panels with thin gray borders and uniform white gutters. Reading order
is Z-pattern.

Above the grid, a short hand-lettered title: The Quote Has Baggage

Top Left panel (Setup): a simple desk outline with a microphone stand suggests a podcast. A stick
figure with round glasses (Expert) speaks into a handheld microphone. Beside him on the desk stands
a large rectangular quote card: at its left, the short black printed words "AI could…", and then a
wide, completely empty white area filling the rest of the card. Speech bubble from the Expert:
"AI could…"

Top Right panel (Development): a single ground line and a waist-high pallet of plain cans. A stick
figure in a striped pom-pom wool cap (Survivalist) stands at a small table. On the table lies the
same quote card, still reading only "AI could…" followed by empty white space. Next to it is a
luggage-tag-shaped note with a string loop: a black header "MY TAKE:" and under it the orange
handwritten words "empty stores." He holds a marker whose tip touches the last orange letter, and
with his other hand aims a smartphone camera at the card and the tag together. The two on-image
texts are the card "AI could…" and the tag "MY TAKE: empty stores." No speech bubble.

Bottom Left panel (Turn, the strongest color emphasis): an airport baggage carousel shown as a
simple curved belt outline. The small "MY TAKE" luggage tag from the previous panel lies alone and
detached on the belt, its header facing down so no text shows. A stick figure holding a clipboard
(Inspector) has a received card clipped to the clipboard reading, in one line, black "AI could"
followed directly by orange "empty stores." With the other hand the Inspector ties on a new, larger
luggage tag of their own: black header "MY TAKE:" and big orange handwritten words "jobs are next."
The clipboard card reads "AI could empty stores." and the new tag reads "MY TAKE: jobs are next."
No travelers or other people.

Bottom Right panel (Conclusion): back at the podcast desk. Behind it a tall wall of stacked plain
cans. The Expert with round glasses holds up the original quote card, still "AI could…" followed by
empty white space, and looks sideways at a large received card on the desk that reads, black
"AI could" then orange "empty stores. Jobs are next." The Survivalist's pom-pom wool cap and eyes
peek over the top of the can wall, silent. Speech bubble from the Expert: "My unfinished sentence
checked two bags."
