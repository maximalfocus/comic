# Cover 01: 请联系上下文 (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 请联系上下文 comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip: one
wide, calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band so
a 2.35:1 center crop keeps the handout, the rice sacks, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a fully opaque, solid pure white (#FFFFFF) background with no
transparency, and a minimal palette of black, white, and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used only
for the handwritten fill-in marks. No shading, gradients, hatching, background fills, motion or
speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Generous blank white negative space at the very top
and very bottom, with all content in the central horizontal band.

Left-to-right composition across the middle: a long paper handout with a black underlined blank
that is completely empty and white; from that empty blank, three small orange handwritten
scribble-lines (wordless squiggles, no letters) drift to the right, each one longer than the last,
toward a growing stack of plain black-and-white rice sacks with no writing on them. A simple
circle-head stick figure holding a smartphone peeks out from behind the rice sacks.

On the left side, in the central band above the handout, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified
Chinese: 请联系上下文, in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly
legible.

The only text in the image is the title "请联系上下文". Keep everything else wordless. Characters stay
black and white; orange appears only on the wordless scribble-lines.
