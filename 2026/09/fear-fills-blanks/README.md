# fear-fills-blanks

Idea: 恐惧会顺着留白传播. Episode 2 of the `trust-per-link` series.

## Shared thread
A vague sentence leaves a pure white blank. Each listener sincerely writes their own fear in as
"my reading"; in transmission that ownership label is lost or upgraded, the next listener believes
it and adds their own fear, and the grown sentence comes to stand for what the expert said. The
ending never reveals what the blank really held. Orange marks only the words listeners filled in;
the episode-1 cast and hoard return.

## How the two differ
- English: quote baggage. The Survivalist's "MY TAKE" luggage tag falls off at a baggage carousel,
  the Inspector clips on a tag of their own, and the Expert's unfinished sentence "checked two bags".
- Chinese: 教辅 answer 接龙 in the family group. 舅舅's 「我的答案」 becomes 「参考答案」, 外甥女
  believes it and adds more, and it ends as 「标准答案」 — 「专家终于说全了」.
