# hit-bobing-zhuangyuan

Chinese-only comic, episode 3 of the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series (episode 1:
../hit-noon-moon, episode 2: ../hit-mooncake-offcenter). Not posted.

Idea: at 博饼 the engineer rolls 状元 first, states the rule that only a bigger 状元 can take it,
survives the last roll, and then the waiter clearing the bowl for dessert jostles the dice into
状元插金花 (15/46656, 万分之三) and takes the prize "by the rules". The waiter wins again, as in
episode 2. Accent: orange on the red dice pips and the token prize card. Drafts and rejected
versions: ../hit-noon-moon/ep3-ideas.md.
