**Turn:** the break-in gets recycled as the reason not to report it — good manners promoted to
compliance evidence, two months before anyone outside hears about it.

**Payoff:** the only thing that forced disclosure was a reporter's phone call, and the first
question is who leaked, not what happened.

## Facts asserted beyond the source
- The model was never reported to have been told it was in a sandbox, or to have called it a drill.
- Three doors stands in for three companies; the `REPO` key is the method in two of the three
  cases (the third was password guessing).
- No stamped document or internal "nothing to disclose" scene is reported; the source says Google
  knew in July and did not disclose until the WSJ reached out.
- The phone call is staged; the source says the WSJ reached out, presumably on a tip, and does not
  quote anyone asking who told them.
