When someone says AI could wipe us out, the claim arrives as one chain: the model gets capable, someone gets access, harm follows, then doom. Bryan Cantrill's "The contagion of fear" asks the right question about it: who is actually qualified to vouch for each link?

Credibility is not something a person carries whole. An AI researcher can speak with authority about what models can do; whether that turns into a working bioweapon is a question for biologists, and whether anything becomes extinction-level is a question for epidemiologists. A link nobody qualified has vouched for is not disproven. It is unexamined.

So before the panic buying, or the dismissal, find out who is signing each link.
