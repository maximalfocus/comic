# Cover 01: 这条因果，谁盖章？ (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 这条因果，谁盖章？ comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip:
one wide, calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band
so a 2.35:1 center crop keeps the approval form, its stamp, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background; the whole image is fully opaque with a
solid white page background and no transparency. A minimal palette of black, white, and a single
spot orange (#FF6B35) used only for the approval form and its one stamp. No shading, gradients,
hatching, background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Generous blank
white negative space at the very top and very bottom, with all content in the central horizontal
band.

On the right two-thirds, a long horizontal orange paper form with four empty boxes in a row. Only
the leftmost box carries one round orange seal stamp (drawn as a plain ring with no lettering); the
other three boxes each hold one large black question mark. A simple circle-head stick figure with
dot eyes stands beside the form holding a large rubber stamp, looking at the three unstamped boxes,
unsure where to press it.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese:
这条因果，谁盖章？, in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "这条因果，谁盖章？". Keep everything else wordless: the boxes
and the seal carry no letters. Characters stay black and white; orange appears only on the form and
its seal.
