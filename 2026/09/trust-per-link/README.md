# trust-per-link

Idea: 可信度不是一个人整体拥有的，而是分散在因果链的每一环上。

## Shared thread
A frightening claim arrives as one orange object, then the turn exposes its separate causal links.
Only the link inside the speaker's field has a qualified voucher; the others remain open questions,
not false links. Orange marks concept objects and their short labels only.

## How the two differ
- English: expertise works like a warranty on a chain. A survivalist discovers that the confident
  sales pitch covers only one link, then tries to return an absurd pallet of beans.
- Chinese: credibility becomes a familiar multi-department approval form. A family-group uncle
  discovers that only one box carries a stamp, then learns that returning the rice is the supermarket's
  link.
