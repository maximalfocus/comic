Chinese-only comic, episode 4 of the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series (episodes 1–3:
../hit-noon-moon, ../hit-mooncake-offcenter, ../hit-bobing-zhuangyuan). Not posted. No waiter.

minutes, no one named). The young engineer (2015级) freezes in front of a 1982级 alumnus, blurts
「同学」, and the old man, called a classmate for the first time in forty years, instantly asks to
copy homework. Accent: orange only on the name badges. Drafts: ../hit-noon-moon/ep4-ideas.md.
