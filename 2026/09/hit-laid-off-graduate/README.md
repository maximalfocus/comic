# hit-laid-off-graduate

Chinese-only comic, episode 10 of the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series (after the
../hit-sixty-pass-line special). Not posted.

Idea: the angle is the value and opportunity the alumni association brings, as career
development. 毕业 means both graduating and, in tech slang, being laid off. At sign-in the
engineer says he 毕业 last week and the student volunteer gives him the 20-SGD student price; he
was laid off. At 博饼 the table makes him the 状元 prize; at 14:30 he walks back with a business
card reading 「周一报到」 and pays the 80 difference because he is no longer a student. Accent:
orange on the price board, the prize medal, and the card. Drafts and rejected versions:
../hit-noon-moon/value-ideas.md.
