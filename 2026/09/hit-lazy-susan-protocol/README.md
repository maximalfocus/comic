# hit-lazy-susan-protocol

Chinese-only comic, episode 11 of the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series. Not posted.

Idea: a graduate student at NTU, stuck for three months on robots colliding in a narrow doorway,
finds research inspiration at the alumni lunch: ten people share one lazy susan and never
collide, because nobody spins it while someone is picking food and elders go first. He writes the
etiquette down; on Monday his robots no longer collide, but they bow to each other at the door
forever: 「您先请。」「您先请。」 Accent: orange on the badge, the lazy susan, and the napkin.
Echoes episode 2's 「学长您先请」. Drafts and rejected versions:
../hit-noon-moon/research-ideas.md.
