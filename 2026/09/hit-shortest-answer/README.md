# hit-shortest-answer

Chinese-only comic, episode 7 of the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series (episodes 1–6 in the sibling
hit-* themes of 2026/09). Not posted. No waiter.

Idea: at the 13:00 Kahoot, a table of engineers works every question out while the 1982级 alumnus
taps without reading and tops the leaderboard. His method is the exam rhyme 「三长一短选最短」, and
his parting 「你们当年，不也这么蒙的？」 exposes the young engineers, whose generation the rhyme
belongs to. The first draft's 「我考哈工大，就靠这句」 was dropped: it belittled the older
generation's entrance exam and misdates the rhyme. Accent: orange only on the shortest answer bar
and the leaderboard's first place. Drafts: ../hit-noon-moon/ep7-ideas.md.
