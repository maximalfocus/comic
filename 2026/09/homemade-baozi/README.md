# homemade-baozi — Chinese only

Source: a wife's WeChat messages about a day spent making 包子 (「一早忙到现在，你又难过了吧」
「活着就是为了吃」). The husband would rather she rest and read than wear herself out on chores.

The comic grants his wish literally: she does read from dawn — a cookbook,《包子大全》— and spends
the day making 包子. Her own line becomes the punchline; her motto is the caption.

Chinese-only by request; no English version.
