# Cover 01: 域名在给谁指路？ (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 域名在给谁指路？ comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip:
one wide, calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band
so a 2.35:1 center crop keeps the signpost, the alley, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background and a minimal palette of black, white,
and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used only for the arrow signs. No shading, gradients, hatching,
background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Generous blank white
negative space at the very top and very bottom, with all content in the central horizontal band.

Centered composition: a simple crossroads drawn with a few clean lines, and in its middle one tall
wooden signpost carrying five blank orange arrow signs pointing in different directions. Four arrows
point toward plain little shops along the road; one arrow points toward a narrow black-and-white
alley on the right whose entrance shows a small skull icon. A simple circle-head stick figure with
dot eyes stands at the foot of the signpost, looking up at the arrows, unsure which way to go.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese:
域名在给谁指路？, in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "域名在给谁指路？". Keep everything else wordless: the arrow
signs are blank. Characters stay black and white; orange appears only on the arrow signs.
