# Storyboard: What Is DNS For?

- **Dialogue language:** English
- **Accent color:** orange `#FF6B35`, used only on domain-name tags, the tally board, and the gate. Never on characters. Panel 3 has the strongest accent; the gate has only a small orange latch. Keep the page 90%+ black and white.
- **Page layout:** landscape 4:3; exactly four equal panels in a strict 2×2 grid with uniform white gutters. Z order: 1 top-left, 2 top-right, 3 bottom-left, 4 bottom-right. Title and caption outside the grid, with no extra panels.
- **Drawing and text:** clean uniform black line art on white; circle-head stick figures, dot eyes, single-line mouths, 4–5 heads tall. Mostly blank backgrounds with counter outlines; no shading, hatching, gradients, background fills, sound effects, or motion lines. Dialogue in simple thin-outlined speech bubbles. Each panel has exactly the two short on-image lines specified below, including object labels; no additional writing on props.
- **Roles (3):** the registrar clerk (visor cap, behind a counter); the honest baker (chef's hat); the scammers (sunglasses, a queue of identical figures count as one role)
- **Title above the grid:** What Is DNS For?
- **Caption below the grid:** The purpose of a system is what it does.
