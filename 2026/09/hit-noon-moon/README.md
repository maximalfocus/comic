# hit-noon-moon

Chinese-only comic (no English version by the user's choice; not posted).

Idea: a daytime "赏明月" (11:00–14:30, Singapore) meets HIT engineers who compute the moon's
position (−28° at noon; moonrise 14:06 SGT per PyEphem for Keng Lee Rd) and fix the missing moon
by bringing mooncakes; the real moon arrives after they've eaten. Accent: orange on mooncakes and
moon. The satellite line nods to 哈工大造卫星; the caption carries the 联欢会 date and venue.
