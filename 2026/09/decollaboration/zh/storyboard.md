# Storyboard: 三人行

- Title (above the strip): 三人行 — evokes 《论语》"三人行，必有我师焉", paid off by the caption
- Dialogue language: Chinese (zh), full-width punctuation, rounded hand-lettered comic style
- Accent color: orange #FF6B35 — only on the connecting threads and thread spools; strongest in
  the turn panel. Characters, laptops, screens, and the 保温杯 stay black and white.
- Layout: vertical 1×4 strip (9:16). Panels stacked top to bottom in 起承转合 order.
- Roles: 研究生 (round glasses); 师兄 (保温杯 thermos flask); 师姐 (hair bun with a pencil in it)
- Mirror staging: panel 3 exactly repeats panel 1's overhead composition and seat positions;
  panel 4 repeats panel 2's side view, with 师兄's chair empty and only his 保温杯 left
- Caption (below the strip): 三人行必有我师，可谁都不问谁了。

## Panels (起承转合)

1. **Top — 起 Setup.** 组会. Overhead view of one round table: 研究生 at the bottom, 师兄 upper
   left, 师姐 upper right. Three taut orange threads run between their hands, forming a triangle
   over the blank center. 师兄 points at 研究生's paper.
   - 师兄："你这个假设站不住。"
   - 研究生："唉，那你说哪不对？"
2. **Upper-middle — 承 Development.** Side view of the same table: 研究生 left, 师兄 right with
   his 保温杯. One orange thread spans the table. Beside a black-and-white laptop, 研究生 turns
   the crank of a small orange spool, winding in their end; 师兄 still holds the far end.
   - 研究生："问AI，不欠人情，也不挨怼。"
3. **Lower-middle — 转 Turn, strongest orange.** Corner label "后来". Exact overhead repeat of
   panel 1, all three in the same seats, each looking into their own black-and-white laptop. No
   thread crosses the table; three large full orange spools sit one beside each laptop.
   - All three (one shared bubble): "效率拉满！"
4. **Bottom — 合 Conclusion.** Same side view as panel 2. 研究生 alone before the laptop; 师兄's
   chair empty, only the 保温杯 remains. The spool beside the laptop is swollen with thread.
   研究生 looks uncertainly at the screen.
   - 研究生："还有我没发现的漏洞吗？"
   - 电脑："有，都在你没问的地方。"
