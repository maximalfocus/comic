# Cover 01: 三人行 (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 三人行 comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip: one wide,
calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band so a
2.35:1 center crop keeps the table, the threads, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background and a minimal palette of black, white,
and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used only for the connecting threads. No shading, gradients,
hatching, background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Generous blank
white negative space at the very top and very bottom, with all content in the central horizontal
band.

Centered composition: a round meeting table seen slightly from above, drawn as a simple ellipse
spanning the middle of the banner. Around it, three simple circle-head stick figures with dot eyes
and single-line mouths are seated, and one chair is conspicuously empty. Thin orange threads and a
small orange spool rest on the table, linking the seated figures to one another; one thread is
loose, its end trailing toward the empty chair. One of the seated figures is turned away from the
others toward a small glowing rectangular screen, ignoring the threads.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese: 三人行,
in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "三人行". Keep everything else wordless. Characters stay
black and white; orange appears only on the threads and spool.
