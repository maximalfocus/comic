# Storyboard: One Thread at a Time

- Title (above grid): One Thread at a Time
- Dialogue language: English
- Accent color: orange #FF6B35 — only on the connecting threads and thread spools; laptop screens remain black and white
- Roles: Researcher (glasses), Colleague A (coffee mug), Colleague B (scarf)
- Mirror staging: panel 3 exactly repeats panel 1's overhead composition and fixed figure positions; panel 4 repeats panel 2's camera and table layout, replacing Colleague A with an empty chair

## Panels (起承转合)

1. **Top left — Setup (起).** Overhead view of one round table: Researcher sits at bottom, Colleague A at upper left, and Colleague B at upper right. Three taut orange threads run directly between their near hands, forming a clear triangular web over the blank center of the table. Colleague A points at Researcher's paper.
   - Colleague A: "Your assumption is wrong."
   - Researcher: "Ugh. Fine, let's argue."

2. **Top right — Development (承).** Side view of Researcher and Colleague A across the same table, with the coffee mug beside Colleague A. The orange thread still connects their near hands. Beside a black-and-white laptop, Researcher calmly winds their end of that thread around a small orange hand-crank spool. Colleague A still holds the far end.
   - Researcher: "No arguments. No credit disputes."

3. **Bottom left — Turn (转), strongest orange.** Corner label "Later". Exact overhead repeat of panel 1: same round table and all three figures in the same fixed positions. Each now looks into a separate black-and-white laptop. No thread spans the blank space between them. Instead, the three intact orange threads are fully wound onto three large orange spools, one beside each laptop. The three full spools are the largest orange shapes in the strip.
   - All three (one shared bubble): "So productive!"

4. **Bottom right — Conclusion (合).** Same side view, table, and object positions as panel 2. Researcher sits before the same black-and-white laptop. Across the table, Colleague A's chair is empty and the coffee mug remains. The orange spool beside Researcher's laptop is swollen with neatly wound thread. Researcher looks uncertainly at the screen.
   - Researcher: "Any flaws I missed?"
   - Laptop: "Only the ones you didn't ask about."

- Caption (below grid): Nobody quit collaborating. They just stopped asking one another.
