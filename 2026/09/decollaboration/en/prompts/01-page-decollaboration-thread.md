# Page 01: One Thread at a Time

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: four-panel | Aspect: 4:3

## Character Reference
- Researcher: circle-head stick figure wearing round glasses. Black and white.
- Colleague A: circle-head stick figure with a coffee mug. Black and white.
- Colleague B: circle-head stick figure wearing a scarf. Black and white.
- Faces: dot eyes, single-line mouth. No detailed faces.

## Panel Breakdown
1. Top Left (Setup): overhead view of a round table; three figures linked by three taut orange threads forming a triangle; Colleague A points at Researcher's paper. "Your assumption is wrong." / "Ugh. Fine, let's argue."
2. Top Right (Development): side view; Researcher winds their end of the orange thread onto a small orange hand-crank spool beside a black-and-white laptop; Colleague A still holds the far end. "No arguments. No credit disputes."
3. Bottom Left (Turn): same overhead view as panel 1; each figure faces their own laptop; no threads between them; three large full orange spools beside the laptops. Label "Later". "So productive!"
4. Bottom Right (Conclusion): same side view as panel 2; Colleague A's chair empty except the coffee mug; Researcher's spool swollen with thread. "Any flaws I missed?" / "Only the ones you didn't ask about."
