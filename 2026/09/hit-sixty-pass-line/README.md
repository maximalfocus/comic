# hit-sixty-pass-line

Chinese-only celebration comic in the HIT Singapore alumni 中秋 series. Not posted.

Idea: 60 alumni have signed up and joined the group, which is exactly the plan's minimum of 10
tables × 6. The engineer celebrates 「60分万岁」, a senior points at the motto 「规格严格　功夫到家」
— HIT has no 60分万岁 — and everyone starts a 「补考」: each brings one more classmate. The caption
invites more sign-ups. No seat limit is stated, since it is unconfirmed. Accent: orange on the
score, the motto banner and the forwarded posters. Drafts: ../hit-noon-moon/celebrate-60-ideas.md.
