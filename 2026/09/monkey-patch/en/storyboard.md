# Storyboard: Monkey Patch

- Title (above the grid): Monkey Patch
- Dialogue language: English
- Accent color: orange #FF6B35, only on the FAIL/PASS labels and the fake clock patch; strongest
  in panel 3. Characters stay black and white.
- Roles: Developer (glasses); Monkey (monkey-ear headband, clip-on curly tail); Townsfolk (crowd)
- Caption (below the grid): Patch a shared clock, and its readers see the fake time.

## Panels

1. **Top left — 起 Setup.** Developer sits at a desk with a laptop showing an orange FAIL label.
   Through one window, the town clock tower's analog clock shows 9:00 (hands only, no digits).
   Line — Developer: "This test only passes at midnight."
2. **Top right — 承 Development.** Same desk and window. The laptop now shows an orange PASS
   label. Out the window, the monkey on the clock tower presses an orange analog clock face,
   hands pointing to midnight, no digits, over the real face.
   Line — Developer: "It passes!"
3. **Bottom left — 转 Turn.** Small black sun icon and corner label "9:05 AM". The orange fake
   clock face, hands still at midnight, dominates the frame on the tower; a crowd of townsfolk
   below points up at it.
   Line — Townsfolk: "Why is it midnight?"
4. **Bottom right — 合 Conclusion.** The monkey stands by the tower holding the removed orange
   patch; the uncovered real clock's hands show 9:05, no digits. The developer nods.
   Line — Monkey: "Patch it, test it, peel it off."
