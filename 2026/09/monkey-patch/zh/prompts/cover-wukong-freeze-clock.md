# Cover 01: 猴子补丁 (公众号 封面, 2.35:1 banner)

## Visual Style
Art: minimalist | Tone: neutral | Layout: single wide banner | Aspect: 16:9 (cropped to 2.35:1)

## Purpose
A dedicated WeChat 公众号 lead cover for the 猴子补丁 comic. It is NOT a panel of the strip: one wide,
calm horizontal key-visual, with all important content held in the central horizontal band so a
2.35:1 center crop keeps 悟空, the clock, and the title.

## Generation Prompt

A minimalist, clean line-art digital illustration as one wide horizontal banner. Simplified cartoon
style with clear black outlines on a pure white background and a minimal palette of black, white,
and a single spot orange (#FF6B35) used only for the paper talisman. No shading, gradients,
hatching, background fills, motion or speed lines, emphasis marks, or sound effects. Generous blank
white negative space at the very top and very bottom, with all content in the central horizontal
band.

Centered composition: a plain office wall with a round analog punch clock whose hands point exactly
at nine o'clock (no digits on the face). Beside it, 悟空 — a simple stick-figure monkey with the
classic 金箍 hoop with two upturned curls at the forehead, small round ears, a long curly tail, and a
short tiger-skin wrap — rides a small 筋斗云 cloud and presses an orange paper talisman onto the
clock face. Below, a simple circle-head stick figure with round glasses sits at a desk with a
laptop, looking up at the clock.

On the left side, in the central band, a short hand-lettered title in Simplified Chinese: 猴子补丁,
in a rounded, thicker comic style (like a soft marker), large and clearly legible.

The only text in the image is the title "猴子补丁" and the single character "定" on the talisman.
Keep everything else wordless. Characters stay black and white; orange appears only on the
talisman.
