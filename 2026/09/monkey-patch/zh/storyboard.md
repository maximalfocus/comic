# Storyboard: 猴子补丁

- Title (above the grid): 猴子补丁
- Dialogue language: Chinese (zh), full-width punctuation
- Accent color: orange #FF6B35, only on the "定" talisman patch and the 失败/通过 screen labels;
  strongest in panel 3. Characters stay black and white.
- Roles: 程序员 (glasses); 悟空 (classic 金箍 hoop with two upturned curls at the forehead,
  small round monkey ears, long curly tail, short tiger-skin wrap, 金箍棒 with a band at each
  end; rides a 筋斗云 cloud in panel 2); 同事们 (crowd with backpacks)
- Caption (below the grid): 补丁贴在大家共用的钟上：谁贴的，谁来揭。

## Panels

1. **Top left — 起 Setup.** Office. 程序员 at a desk; monitor shows orange "失败". On the wall, an
   analog 打卡钟 (punch clock) with hands a few minutes before 9, no digits.
   Line — 程序员："打卡测试只有九点整才能过。"
2. **Top right — 承 Development.** 悟空 rides a small 筋斗云 up to the wall and slaps an orange
   paper talisman bearing "定" onto the punch clock; its hands stop at 9:00. Monitor shows orange "通过".
   Lines — 悟空："定！" / 程序员："过了！"
3. **Bottom left — 转 Turn.** Window shows night: a crescent moon; corner label "18:30". The punch
   clock, orange "定" talisman still on it, hands at 9:00, dominates. Colleagues with backpacks
   queue in front of it, staring.
   Line — 同事们："天都黑了，怎么还是九点？！"
4. **Bottom right — 合 Conclusion.** 悟空 peels the talisman off; the clock hands show 6:30.
   Colleagues hurry out the door with their backpacks. 程序员 looks sheepish.
   Lines — 悟空："解！" / 程序员："定完要记得解……"
